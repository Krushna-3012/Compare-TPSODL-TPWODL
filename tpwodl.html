TPWODL

About the Company

TP Western Odisha Distribution Limited (TPWODL) is a joint venture between The Tata Power Company Limited and the Government of Odisha, with Tata Power holding the majority stake of 51%. The distribution company operates retail supply across the western region of the state.

TPWODL maintains extensive overhead network systems to distribute power, which is procured from GRIDCO at 33kV at multiple supply points. The operational area spans nine revenue districts, structured systematically under 5 Circles and 17 Electrical Divisions to ensure prompt consumer query resolution and reliable distribution services.

In alignment with its modernization policies, TPWODL has deployed unique digital systems for consumers, including advanced Geographic Information Systems (GIS), specialized outage calculators, and multiple online billing options.
