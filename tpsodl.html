About the Company

TP Southern Odisha Distribution Limited (TPSODL) is a public-private partnership joint venture between The Tata Power Company Limited, holding a 51% majority stake, and the Government of Odisha (49%).

The company focuses on the distribution and retail supply of electricity in Southern Odisha. TPSODL is committed to introducing advanced digital tools, systematic upgrades to overhead lines, and reducing Aggregate Technical & Commercial (AT&C) losses across the electrical network to improve reliability and prompt customer query redressal.

With a field workforce exceeding 15,000 employees, TPSODL manages an expansive network of distribution grids, mobile substations, and Area Power System Control Centres (APSCC) for efficient load dispatch and outage restoration.

Renewable Energy & Solar Schemes

Key programs administered by TPSODL to encourage clean solar energy integration.

PM KUSUM Component-C

As the regional implementation agency, TPSODL supports the solarization of agricultural pumps up to 7.5 HP.

  • 1**Financial Structure:** 30% Central subsidy (MNRE), 30% State Govt subsidy, and 40% farmer contribution (bank finance available).
  • 2**Objectives:** Provides reliable daytime irrigation, reduces grid load, and allows selling surplus power back.

Solar Rooftop Scheme

Enables residential, institutional, and commercial consumers to build Solar PV systems.

  • 1**Matikhala Solarization:** Complete solarization of Matikhala village in Ganjam district under PM Surya Ghar.
  • 2**Solar Calculator:** Integrated online calculator tool to compute load size, area required, and financial paybacks.
